## Brand & Style

This design system serves the Comunidade Cristã Umbandista (C.C.U.), a respected and welcoming Umbanda spiritual center based in Brasília/DF. The visual atmosphere balances sacred reverence, institutional trust, and warm community hospitality. Rather than falling into esoteric cliches or overly dense ritualistic graphics, the aesthetic embraces modern institutional clarity—dignified, transparent, and spiritually comforting.

The design movement combines **Corporate / Modern Institutional** discipline with **Minimalism and subtle sacred depth**:
- **Clarity & Serenity:** Ample breathing room, immaculate typography, and luminous off-white reading grounds reflect spiritual purity, peace, and order.
- **Dignity & Tradition:** Deep navy blue anchors the visual presence with authority, stability, and contemplation, evoking the solemnity of the night sky and spiritual guardianship.
- **Vitality & Devotion:** Ruby/crimson red provides warmth, energetic highlights, actionable urgency, and institutional identity, derived directly from the outer circle of the house's insignia.
- **Tone of Voice:** Reverent yet welcoming, orderly, fraternal, and deeply community-oriented.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the official circular emblem of the Comunidade Cristã Umbandista:

- **Primary (`#0F1F38` - Deep Navy Blue):** The primary brand anchor representing spiritual guidance, institutional dignity, and grounded depth. Used for dominant headings, navigation surfaces, high-contrast borders, and institutional emblems.
- **Secondary (`#C51E28` - Ruby Crimson):** The sacred vitality accent derived from the insignia's ring. Used intentionally for high-importance calls to action, active statuses, liturgical calendar badges, and devotional focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#1E3A8A` - Royal Star Navy):** Derived from the core five-pointed star in the emblem. Used for interactive states, secondary links, and mid-tone accents.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Neutral):** Provides balanced optical weight for supporting metadata, iconography, and subtle borders.
- **Backgrounds & Canvas:** High-legibility backgrounds built on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) layered over soft cool off-white (`#F8FAFC`) to maximize comfort during lengthy textual study (doctrinal texts, session schedules, and hymns).
- **Subtle Signature Gradient:** A 135-degree linear transition from `#0F1F38` to `#C51E28` reserved exclusively for hero banners, ceremonial badges, header divider strokes, and decorative accents.

## Typography

The typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines with **Inter** for body text and functional UI controls:

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its balanced geometry, humane warmth, and clear architectural stability. It communicates confidence and accessibility without severity, matching the communal and open nature of Umbanda.
- **Body & Functional (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and engineered for high-density reading across screen sizes. Ideal for spiritual notices, educational studies, bylaws, and gira (ceremony) calendars.
- **Hierarchy & Tracking:** Display titles apply slight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) for cohesive visual weight, while functional labels and micro-indicators feature open letter-spacing (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to maintain sharp legibility when rendered in small caps or badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is organized around a 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a max container width of `1280px` to maintain optimal line lengths for reading and contemplative discovery:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) side margins. Informational blocks, such as ceremonial calendars, doctrinal archives, and contact info, span predictable 4, 6, 8, or 12 column cards.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) margins. Content stacks naturally into dual columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins. Cards reflow into a clean, vertical reading stream.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical spacing (`space-xl` = 40px) between thematic sections enforces calm and orderly pacing, avoiding cluttered visuals and facilitating quiet reflection.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs serene, ambient depth rather than artificial or stark layering:

- **Surface Tonal Stacking:** Baseline pages rest on `#F8FAFC` (Cool Off-White). Functional modules, cards, and reading containers are elevated using pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Ambient Shadows:** Shadows are diffused, soft, and tinted with deep navy pigment instead of harsh black:
  - *Elevation 1 (Cards, Rest State):* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 31, 56, 0.06), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(15, 31, 56, 0.04)`.
  - *Elevation 2 (Hover State, Dropdowns):* `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(15, 31, 56, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 31, 56, 0.03)`.
  - *Elevation 3 (Modals, Overlays):* `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 31, 56, 0.14), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 31, 56, 0.05)`.
- **Hairline Dividers & Outlines:** Low-contrast ghost borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) frame cards and navigation surfaces, reinforcing structure without visual weight.
- **Gradient Accents:** Ceremonial headers and key highlight strips use a delicate navy-to-ruby gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0F1F38 0%, #C51E28 100%)`) with low surface elevation to maintain institutional sobriety.

## Shapes

The design uses discreet, refined rounded corners (`roundedness: 2`):

- **Default UI Elements (`8px / 0.5rem`):** Applied to buttons, text inputs, chips, alert banners, and small thumbnail containers. This curvature softens the interface while preserving institutional dignity.
- **Cards & Content Blocks (`rounded-lg: 16px / 1rem`):** Used for schedule cards, notice containers, and prayer request modules.
- **Modals & Flyouts (`rounded-xl: 24px / 1.5rem`):** Reserved for dialogs and hero card containers.
- **Circular Elements:** Fully rounded shapes (`50%` / pill-shaped) are strictly reserved for the CCU circular logo emblem, status indicator dots, avatars, and floating action badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid Deep Navy (`#0F1F38`) background with crisp white text, `8px` corner radius, `space-sm` vertical and `space-md` horizontal padding. On hover, background shifts to `#162447` with Elevation 2.
- **Accent / Donation Button:** Crimson Red (`#C51E28`) background with crisp white text. Used selectively for urgent actions, spiritual reception check-in, or voluntary support.
- **Secondary / Outline Button:** 1.5px solid border in `#0F1F38` with transparent background and navy text. Subtle `#0F1F38`/5% background tint on hover.
- **Ghost Button:** Transparent background, slate text (`#475569`), hover state adopts `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Category Badges:** `4px` padding vertical, `10px` horizontal with `8px` corner radius. 
  - *Ceremonial / Gira:* Crimson Red background at 10% opacity with `#C51E28` text and a subtle 1px border.
  - *Doctrinal / Study:* Navy background at 8% opacity with `#0F1F38` text.
  - *Public / Open Session:* Forest emerald tint (`#065F46` on `#ECFDF5`).

### Cards & Content Modules
- Built on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border and `rounded-lg` (16px) corners.
- Featured session cards feature a 3px top accent bar utilizing the signature navy-to-crimson gradient.
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg` / 24px) guarantees comfort when reviewing ritual hours, clothing guidelines, and spiritual guidance information.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px, `rounded` (8px), border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Focus state applies a `2px` ring in `#1E3A8A` with zero offset.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px` square/circle, `#0F1F38` fill when selected with pure white checkmark. Focus outlines share the `#1E3A8A` ring.

### Lists & Calendars
- **Gira & Event Items:** Divided by fine `1px solid #F1F5F9` lines. Each entry highlights the scheduled time in bold deep navy, accompanied by a ruby crimson date badge, title, presiding medium, and public admittance status.

### Specialized Spiritual Components
- **Ceremonial Notice Banner:** Subdued navy container with a subtle star watermark and white text, announcing emergency closings or festival days (Festa de Preto Velho, Iemanjá, Ogum).
- **Hymn & Literature Viewer:** Serif or high-clarity sans body layout with expanded line height (`1.75`), generous left-hand indentation, and soft gray quotation borders for chants, ponto cantados, and prayers.
